# Safety

Blind Helper is an experimental engineering and research project.

The current prototype is not a certified mobility aid or medical device.

The system may fail to detect an obstacle.

Possible causes include:

- Low optical reflectivity
- Transparent surfaces
- Highly reflective surfaces
- Sensor misalignment
- Environmental infrared interference
- Electrical failure
- Battery failure
- Software failure
- Incorrect mounting
- Objects outside the sensor field of view

The device must therefore not replace established mobility aids such as a white cane or guide dog.

Prototype testing with blind or visually impaired participants should only take place after controlled engineering validation and with appropriate safety precautions.
